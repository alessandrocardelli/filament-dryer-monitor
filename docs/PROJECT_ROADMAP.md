## Current verified state and limitations

The first-article 24 V/ESP32 PCB has demonstrated buck/MCU bring-up, manual and bodge-corrected automatic UART programming, push buttons, buzzer, OLED SSD1309, external SHT45, fan and its PWM driver, unloaded MOSFET heater drive, real heater and J7 thermistor path with calibrated ADC. Temporary **standalone** heater tests completed stabilized measurements from 30 through 85 °C at a **nearby Sanwa probe**, while the top and bottom eBOX Lite covers were **open and fan OFF**; the last old-conversion 90 °C indicated diagnostic held for 120 s and measured 7.12 kΩ, Sanwa 85 °C. Thermal system safety is NOT qualified. The **application** firmware continues to hard-lock heater GPIO19 OFF (`firmware/SafetyController.h`) while it runs interface, SHT45 and provisional fan demos.

The original eSUN eBOX Lite manufacturer nominal hot-plate settings are 105/116/118 °C and nominal air temperatures 40/50/55 °C under published test conditions. They are distinct sensor locations and are not certified by current bench measurements. A **105 °C one-shot series TCO** is installed/reported restored but remains potentially incompatible with the original nominal plate range. Its trip has not been independently verified, and it must **not be bypassed**.

The repository includes `hardware/Filament_dryer_pcb.stl` (named PCB STL), but **contains no versioned printable panel/front-bezel CAD** (`.ipt`, `.iam`, `.step`, `.stl` of the new case panel, etc.) at this checkpoint. Do not claim fit/correct mounting of an unseen panel source.

## Priority rules

- The panel **can and should** move forward ahead of full heater characterization; printing is not contingent on dryer operation.
- Keep heater-power tests **out of the critical path for cosmetic/dimensional iterations**.
- Do not confuse an NTC *Sanwa-equivalent* lookup with actual thermistor calibration or heater safety input.
- Avoid unresolved non-MVP extras (Web UI, CSV graphs, hardware V2) from blocking the first mechanically integrated, electrically safe V1.
