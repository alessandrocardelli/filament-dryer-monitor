## Precommitted procedure: attended fan-only, no heater power

**Prerequisites / electrical safety:**
1. With the entire unit **unpowered and unplugged**, check the replacement label: it must be rated **24V** for this 24V fan output. Inspect fan connector and conductor insulation, full physical seating, fan blade/duct clearance, free inlet/outlet and flow-direction arrows. The released board's netlist identifies **J6-2 = +24V_PROT; J6-1 = MOSFET Q5 switched return**, so this board powers a two-wire fan by switching the negative supply: **it does not drive the blue 4-wire PWM input** or read tachometer. For the planned Noctua NF-A4x10 24V PWM, standard Noctua 4-wire colors are **yellow +24V, black return, green tach (unused), blue PWM (unused)**; verify actual model/adapter and wiring first, do not assume. A fitted Low-Noise Adaptor may reduce speed.
2. **Physically disconnect heater connector J5 with power unplugged** and secure/isolate its detached heater harness so no accessible energized contact, accidental short or contact with the PCB is possible. Do not bypass, change, remove or rerate the series **105°C TCO**; leave it in the disconnected heater harness. If safe J5 disconnection/isolation is uncertain, **STOP here** and do not start a cycle. A dashboard HEATER command or `kHeaterOutputEnabled=false` is **not** an electrical guarantee of no heating on this trial firmware.
3. Once all covers and wiring are safely positioned, power up for **cold** telemetry. In STANDBY, note the **actual** displayed build ID if available, SHT45 AIR and RH, ambient-room temperature (if measured), and confirm no unexpected warming. Never reach into the live unit or touch the rotating fan.

**Fan-only execution (J5 must remain electrically disconnected):**
4. With the heater physically disconnected, select a normal **minimum 15min cycle** only to command the fan, press START locally, observe for **60 seconds**, then use the normal **STOP**. The firmware config commands **FAN100%, PWM 25kHz duty255/255** through Q5, with ~1s kickstart and ~5s pre-heat prerun. It may display HEATER ON, but with J5 reliably disconnected there must be **no actual heater energy**; if anything becomes physically warm, stops rotating, rattles/rubs, smells abnormal or a fault appears, STOP and disconnect supply immediately. **Do not reconnect J5 during operation.**
5. At around **10s / 30s / 60s** after START, without opening or reaching into the energized enclosure, record: displayed fan command (%), real fan turning continuously (yes/no), sound/obstruction, visually apparent airflow direction at inlet/outlet (e.g. light tissue strip kept OUTSIDE and away from blades; do not let it be ingested), and AIR°C. This is a **qualitative flow check**, not a measured CFM or RPM qualification. Observe STOP and any post-STOP fan cooldown; no invented exact rpm or pressure values. If desired, provide a brief video from outside. Record firmware ID, date/time (Rome) and that J5 was physically disconnected.
6. **Switch OFF power before any J5 reconnection.** Only reconnect after wiring/connector inspection and a distinct decision to prepare a new hot test; a cold fan PASS is NOT authorization to re-energize the heater.

**Expected output and preservation:** send observations/readings in chat; when the session ends, record actual results and explicitly missing readings in existing `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md`, `docs/TODO.md` and `docs/THERMAL_EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.md`, with raw photos/video/measurements linked where available, in one coherent commit. No fabricated RPM/airflow claims and no per-reading commits.
