## One candidate diagnostic — COLD, NO HEATER

**What:** review/build the isolated sketch [`firmware/diagnostics/ntc_fan_only/ntc_fan_only.ino`](../firmware/diagnostics/ntc_fan_only/ntc_fan_only.ino); it commands **heater GPIO19 LOW at all times** and samples the already calibrated GPIO34 NTC divider. Without touching the Sanwa probe or opening the dryer, it runs fan GPIO16 **20 s OFF → 20 s ON (100% duty, 25 kHz) → 20 s OFF**, one time only, displaying current NTC kΩ and corrected ADC mV on SSD1309 OLED and logging CSV/summary to USB Serial at115200 baud. Average the last 15s in each phase. It cannot perform a drying cycle and replaces the existing application until the original firmware is reflashed. This diagnostic does **not** include Wi-Fi/OTA; USB programming/monitoring may be required. **Do not instruct the operator to flash it before code review/compile and explicit acceptance of this limited test.**

**Why:** a jump in NTC ADC volts/ohms synchronized to fan switching at thermal equilibrium would demonstrate an electrical or supply-coupling measurement issue. If the readings remain stable, this specific cold fan-coupling mechanism becomes less likely but a heater-on disturbance, local heating differences, or Sanwa reading/contact discrepancy would remain open. It is **not** a definitive thermal calibration.

**Connection to prior result / gate:** fan already runs, and both old/new traces begin with plausible room-temperature NTC values. This targeted, non-heating test avoids another unsafe 60min retry and directs the next engineering decision: electrical signal-integrity diagnosis vs independently checking thermal sensor agreement.

**Safety and provenance:** 20/20/20s and 100% fan are `PROVISIONAL_EXPERIMENT` diagnostic parameters (not heater limits). NTC divider constants are `MEASURED_CHARACTERIZATION` from first-article ADC measurements, not a thermistor °C calibration. GPIO19 HEATER never goes HIGH, so no AIR, NTC °C, TCO or manual Sanwa overheat setpoint is exercised in this diagnostic. Physical TCO105 in series must remain unchanged; it is not a validated safe material limit. Prior physical trial was under historical AIR55°C/60min selected settings (`APPROVED_OPERATIONAL` UI settings but `PROVISIONAL_EXPERIMENT` for HEATER), hard 7,120Ω (`PROVISIONAL_EXPERIMENT`), and historical 85°C manual plate veto (`PROVISIONAL_EXPERIMENT`); these do not grant further heating authorization.
