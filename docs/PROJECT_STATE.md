[DNS Club 2026 first-hand test](https://club.dns-shop.ru/amp/review/t-609-aksessuaryi-dlya-3d-pechati/171333-obzor-sushilnoi-kameryi-dlya-filamenta-esun-ebox-lite/) measured **54°C** with K thermocouple between filament strands in a spool after30–40min warmup, stated instrument tolerance±(1%+3 counts). Manufacturer's **118°C hot plate level3** is from a DIFFERENT unspecified measurement point and cannot be interpreted as the plate temperature at our Sanwa contact or as authorization to increase heater exposure. Operator says **the mechanical/heater/fan assembly remained essentially OEM; the PCB was replaced**; treat this as reported, not independent dimensional verification. Independent evidence supports that OEM units can reach near target, but is **NOT** a controlled A/B comparison; different ambient, probe position and spool loading remain possible confounders.

**New user-reported physical finding, 31 October 2026:** After reviewing photographs of the OEM eBOX Lite interior, the operator discovered that **the original radial fan had been mechanically installed backwards** in the prototype during the 31 October AIR55 run (verbal report; no photos of the corrected installation or measured airflow supplied yet). The CSV's 100% FAN is only the PWM command, so previous analyses had no evidence of correct airflow direction. This is a **strong, newly identified potential explanation** for Sanwa PLATE83°C alongside AIR45.84°C during continuous heater ON; the causal effect remains **unconfirmed until orientation is corrected and airflow checked**. Do not assume the heater, NTC, insulation or power is defective on the basis of that run. Existing raw readings and firmware status remain unchanged.
