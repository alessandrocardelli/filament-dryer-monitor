### Derived software correction — UI6 candidate, 26 November

Operator explicitly requested the correction after the seven-point series. Confirmed old device-source `1cd96dd76500dfb4d161e1b445d8df6c9d8e524b` and UI5 use identical electrical parameters: slope 0.984369, offset -2.322mV, effective 3311mV rail and 46572Ω pullup. With no directly sampled node mV, invert the existing divider equation: `legacyMv=3311*reportedOhms/(46572+reportedOhms)`. Compare that estimate to the same equation applied to DMM reference R, then fit a single residual voltage offset. This identifies an effective end-to-end correction, **not an independently isolated physical ADC error**. Equal-weight constant offset 6.004mV: maximum mean error 0.915%, leave-one-resistor-out max 1.067%. Relative-resistance-error weighted constant fit 6.7956mV (weights proportional to squared d ln R/dV at each reference): max mean 0.660%, leave-one-resistor-out max 0.991%; selected rounded 6.8mV. Affine fit (6.937mV intercept, -0.000957 slope) max mean 0.684%, leave-one-out max 0.947%; extra fitted parameter not justified by seven points. No high-order or piecewise fitted curve.
