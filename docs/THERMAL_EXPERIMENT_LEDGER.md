## Interpretation and recording

- Sanwa RD701/K reads local PLATE contact near NTC, not chamber air or the embedded bead itself. Preserve each manual ON/OFF/cooling reading and missing fields; never infer it from CSV. Full records above are authoritative for the readings.
- NTC is heater thermistor resistance. Seven old plateau pairs were obtained with both covers open and fan OFF; they are not a validated bead calibration or closed-case limit.
- SHT45 reads chamber AIR temperature/RH. PWM and heater command fields do not measure RPM, flow or current. A nominal OEM plate reference is not an approved prototype setpoint.
- Software PASS, limited physical observation and production qualification are separate. S7U/STEP08 data acquisition/replay passed within their stated scope; system safety and V1 regulation remain open.
- New tests follow AGENTS: saved WHAT/WHY/PREVIOUS/NEXT protocol and exact source/image; chronological observations, original raw data/hash, missing values, fault/events, analysis and one next decision. No repeats merely to fill already documented historical gaps.
